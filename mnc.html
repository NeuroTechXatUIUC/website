The challenge

What will neuroscience look like 20 years from now?

We have seen brain chips, VR headsets that respond to neural signals, and brain-computer interfaces that support surgical procedures. At NeuroHack, it is your turn to construct or conceptualize what you want to see next in neurotechnology and neuroscience. Bring a technical build, a research-backed concept, or a thoughtful vision for the future.
